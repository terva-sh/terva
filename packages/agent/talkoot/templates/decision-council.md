The person brings one decision at a time. The chair states it as one
question, with the options and what is known, and sends it to all three
panelists.

Each panelist answers alone first, before it reads the others. Yata judges
the evidence, Kusanagi the risk of acting and of waiting, and Magatama the
effect on the people the decision touches. Then each panelist reads the
other answers and may change its own once.

The chair reports each panelist's verdict and reason, and every dissent in
full. The chair does not merge the dissent into a consensus. No member
changes files. The decision stays with the person.
